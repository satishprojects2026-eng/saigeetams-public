# SAI GEETAMs Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 24, 2026

## Overview

SAI GEETAMs ("the App") is a Carnatic music class management application. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- Name (first and last)
- Email address
- Phone number
- Date of birth (students only, for age verification)
- Profile photo (optional)

### Parent/Guardian Information
- Parent/guardian name
- Relationship to student
- Phone number
- Email address
- Emergency contact number

### Class Data
- Attendance records
- Practice logs (duration and notes)
- Skill progress and grades
- Report cards
- Class schedules and session details

### Technical Data
- Push notification tokens (for class reminders)
- Device type (iOS)

## How We Use Your Information

All data collected is used solely for the purpose of managing music classes, including:
- Creating and managing user accounts
- Scheduling and tracking class sessions
- Recording attendance and practice
- Communicating between teachers, students, and parents
- Sending class reminders and notifications
- Generating progress reports and report cards

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

SAI GEETAMs is designed for use by children aged 4 and older under parental supervision.

- We require verifiable parental consent before creating any student account for children under 13
- Parents must provide their information and digitally sign a consent form during registration
- Parent email is verified before any student account is activated
- We do not collect geolocation data from students
- We do not use behavioral advertising
- We do not share student data with third parties for marketing
- Parents can review their child's data at any time through the Parent portal
- Parents can request deletion of their child's data at any time
- We conduct annual privacy reviews and notify parents of any policy changes

## Data Storage and Security

- All data is stored on Supabase (hosted on AWS) with encryption at rest (AES-256)
- All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3
- Student data is only accessible to the assigned teacher, the student, and linked parent/guardian
- No platform administrator has access to student data
- Passwords are hashed using bcrypt
- Student PINs are stored as bcrypt hashes, never in plain text
- Session tokens expire after 30 days with automatic refresh

## Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or rent personal information to any third parties. We do not use:
- Third-party analytics SDKs
- Advertising SDKs
- Data brokers
- Social media tracking

The only external service we use is Apple Push Notification Service for class reminders.

## Data Retention and Deletion

### Account Deletion
- Students can delete their own account (parent PIN confirmation required for children under 13)
- Parents can delete their child's account
- Teachers can delete student accounts from their classes
- Teachers can delete their own account (all associated classes, sessions, and materials are deleted)

### What Happens When an Account is Deleted
- Personal information (name, email, phone, photo) is permanently erased
- Related records are soft-deleted
- Files are permanently removed from storage
- Push notification tokens are deleted
- The deletion is irreversible

## GDPR Compliance

For users in the European Economic Area:
- You have the right to access your personal data
- You have the right to rectify inaccurate data
- You have the right to erasure (right to be forgotten)
- You have the right to data portability
- You can exercise these rights through the app settings or by contacting us

## Changes to This Policy

We will notify users of any material changes to this privacy policy through in-app notifications. Parents will be notified annually for privacy review as required by COPPA.
